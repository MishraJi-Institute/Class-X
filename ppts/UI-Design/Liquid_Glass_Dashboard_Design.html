Liquid Glass Dashboard

Named-slide design presentation
01 · VISION

A study portal that feels like a modern iPhone interface.

A vibrant mesh gradient, layered frosted sheets, and calm motion turn the school library into a premium learning dashboard without sacrificing clarity.

Design goalMake every subject, presentation, and action feel organized, tactile, and easy to discover.
Visual languageGlass, light-catching edges, soft depth, and squircle-like corners.
02 · BACKGROUND

Deep orange, purple, and blue mesh layers.

The background stays expressive while the translucent interface remains readable above it.

OrangeEnergy, warmth, and school activity.
PurpleCreative technology and imagination.
BlueTrust, focus, and academic calm.
03 · GLASS CORE

Every panel behaves like a frosted sheet.

Layered backdrop blur, saturation, translucent fills, and inset highlights create depth while preserving hierarchy.

Layer stackTransparent gradient fill → backdrop-filter blur → crisp top/left bezel → darker bottom/right edge → low-opacity shadow.
FallbackSolid translucent surfaces remain usable when backdrop-filter is unavailable.
04 · BENTO LAYOUT

Information is arranged in clear, reusable blocks.

Uniform 24–32px corners and responsive grid spans make the layout feel like a family of related widgets.

Library gridSubject cards, search, filters, and presentation tiles use the same spacing system.
Responsive12-column desktop grid collapses into a one-column mobile flow.
05 · TYPOGRAPHY

SF-style hierarchy with high contrast.

System fonts create a familiar Apple-like texture. Large headings lead; muted supporting copy explains; compact labels orient.

DisplayTight tracking, strong scale, short lines.
BodyComfortable line-height and soft contrast.
LabelsUppercase, letter-spaced, concise.
06 · INTERACTIONS

Motion should feel physical, not distracting.

Hover lift, soft shadows, and a 98% tap scale mimic the responsive feel of a device surface.

Motion rulesUse a spring-like cubic-bezier, avoid excessive movement, respect reduced-motion settings, and keep touch targets rectangular and clear.
FeedbackButtons compress slightly on press and cards lift gently on hover.
07 · STUDY LIBRARY

AI and future subjects live together.

The GitHub-connected library discovers HTML presentations from ppts/Subject/ and creates the cards automatically.

AIExisting AI Unit 1 presentation is placed in ppts/AI/.
MathematicsFuture Maths HTML PPTs can be dropped into ppts/Mathematics/.
MoreComputer Science, Physics, Chemistry, English, and additional subjects can grow without editing the homepage.
08 · IMPLEMENTATION

One stylesheet. A reusable visual system.

The upgrade is delivered as a standalone liquid-glass.css layer, while library.js handles automatic GitHub discovery.

Filesliquid-glass.css · library.js · animations.js · ppts/AI/ · ppts/UI-Design/
