Chapter 01

Basic Mathematics

The notes begin with foundational algebra and numerical tools. The early pages cover algebraic identities, exponents, surds, inequalities, intervals, modulus and logarithms, followed by short-note style revision material.

Core focus

Algebraic identities • Exponents • Surds • Inequalities • Intervals

Problem tools

Modulus/absolute value • Logarithms • Graph-based reasoning • Case analysis

Chapter 02

Sets

The chapter roadmap moves from definitions and representations of sets to subset/superset ideas, power sets, operations, Venn diagrams, cardinality and Cartesian products.

Set language

Roster form • Set-builder form • Types of sets • Universal set

Operations

Union • Intersection • Difference • Complement • De Morgan's laws • Cardinal number

Chapter 03

Trigonometric Identities

The source roadmap includes angle conventions, standard-angle values, allied and negative-angle formulas, compound angles, double/triple angles, factorization and transformations, followed by graphs of trigonometric functions.

Chapter 04

Trigonometric Equations

Use identities and angle relationships to transform equations into solvable forms, while carefully tracking the complete solution set.

Chapter 05

Complex Numbers — I

A first-stage roadmap for working with complex quantities, algebraic manipulation and problem-solving patterns.

Chapter 06

Quadratic Equations

Focus on quadratic forms, roots and relationships between coefficients and roots, with equation-solving practice.

Chapter 07

Permutation & Combination

Organize counting problems by identifying whether order matters, then build the required counting expression systematically.

Chapter 08

Binomial Theorem

Use binomial expansion structure, general terms and coefficient-based reasoning as a bridge between algebra and combinatorics.

Chapter 09

Sequence & Series

Build fluency with structured numerical patterns, their terms and summation-based reasoning.

Chapter 10

Straight Lines

Coordinate geometry begins with line representations and the algebraic relationships that describe position and slope.

Chapter 11

Circle

Study the geometry of the circle through coordinate relationships and standard equation-based problem solving.

Chapter 12

Develop the coordinate-geometry viewpoint for a parabola and use its standard relationships in problem solving.

Chapter 13

Ellipse

Build the coordinate framework for the ellipse and connect its geometric features with equations.

Chapter 14

Hyperbola

Extend conic-section reasoning to the hyperbola and practice interpreting its coordinate form.

Chapter 15

A second complex-number stage for deeper manipulation and more advanced problem-solving patterns.

Chapter 16

Organize numerical data and work with the statistical ideas included in the Class XI roadmap.

Chapter 17

Solution of Triangle

Use trigonometric relationships to connect sides and angles and solve triangle-based problems systematically.

Revision

17 chapters. One repeatable routine.

Concept → Example

Understand the rule, then see how it behaves in a worked problem.

Example → Practice

Close the notes and reproduce the method independently.

Practice → Error log

Record recurring mistakes and revisit the relevant chapter.

Revision → Mixed set

Finally mix chapters so recognition of the method is tested without a chapter label.
