## 🌿 Shiksha • Sanskriti • Sanskar

The experience is inspired by three simple ideas:

**Shiksha** — Learn with understanding and curiosity.  
**Sanskriti** — Stay connected with values and culture.  
**Sanskar** — Use knowledge responsibly and thoughtfully.

The aim is not only to explain technology, but also to encourage students to think about how knowledge and technology should be used.

## 🤖 Learn Artificial Intelligence

The main learning experience focuses on:

### **Class 10 Artificial Intelligence (417)**
### **Unit 1 — Revisiting AI Project Cycle & Ethical Frameworks for AI**

The material connects concepts with:

- Everyday situations
- Visual explanations
- Diagrams
- Practical examples
- AI applications students encounter around them
- Ethical questions from real life

The presentation contains **67 slides** designed as a visual learning journey.

---

## 💡 From Concept to Real Life

The experience helps students explore questions such as:

**How does an AI system solve a problem?**

**How does AI use data?**

**How does a phone recognize a face?**

**How does a voice assistant understand us?**

**How does translation work?**

**How can AI become biased?**

**Why does privacy matter?**

**What happens when AI is used in healthcare?**

These connections make AI concepts easier to understand and remember.

## 🎓 Made for Students

rather than:

> *“I have to memorize this.”*
